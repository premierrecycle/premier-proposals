Why Totesters

The right equipment for
high-diversion jobsites.

Compact, mobile, and staged by material type directly where trades are working. The result is cleaner source separation, better data, and a safer site from day one.

More Specific Diversion Data

Every cart is individually weighed at Premier's facility, producing per-material diversion data by stream — accurate enough for any corporate sustainability audit.

Better Source Separation

Carts are staged by trade or material type at the point of work, keeping streams clean before they ever leave the floor. No commingling, no sorting after the fact.

Cleaner, Safer Job Sites

Carts roll directly to where trades are working, making debris disposal faster and easier at the source. No hauling material across the floor to a distant bin — workers load the cart where they stand, then roll it out.
