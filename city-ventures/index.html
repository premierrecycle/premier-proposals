Fully Integrated vs. Fragmented

Not all recyclers are the same. Here's why it matters who you choose.

  • ✓
    City-Certified in both San Jose & Los Gatos — our sorting facility meets municipal C&D debris diversion requirements, keeping your projects compliant across all three sites. Diversion reports provide the documentation needed for Environmental Services approval and return of deposit.
  • ✓
    Fully vertically integrated — we own the trucks and the sorting facility. One company, full chain of custody, no subcontractors — diversion tags are produced in-house, no scrambling or calls to third-party facilities.
  • ✓
    San Jose-based — minutes from your sites, fast response, no regional dispatch delays across all three projects.

The Alternative
Hauler-Only Provider
  • ✗
    Out-of-area dispatch — slower response, drivers unfamiliar with your site.
  • ✗
    No CDD program approval — compliance burden falls back on you to verify where material is going.
  • ✗
    Material handed off to whichever facility is cheapest — no downstream ownership, no audit trail.
  • ✗
    Diversion tags require calling multiple facilities to piece together documentation.
  • ✗
    Inconsistent records across facilities — harder to reconcile, harder to defend.
  • ✗
    Calls go to voicemail, tickets go into a queue — you're a job number, not a partner.
What We Offer

Full-Service C&D Recycling, Start to Close-Out

Every part of the recycling process runs through Premier Recycle, from the first bin on site to the last signature on your LEED close-out package.

Debris Bin Rental & Hauling

Roll-off bins in 10, 20, and 40 yard sizes. Dependable pickups, quick turnaround. No surprises. Solutions to your jobsite challenges: covers, pick rings, and Totester mini carts available for tight footprints.

In-House Sorting Facility

The largest hand-sort C&D facility in the U.S., RCI-certified and owned and operated by Premier Recycle. Every load sorted, weighed, and documented under one roof by the same company that picked it up.

Diversion Documentation & Reporting

Diversion reports, waste management plans, and permit recycling refund requests, all produced from independently audited facility data by our on-staff sustainability experts and ready when you need them.

Terms & Conditions
  • Overweight charges: $100/ton for Concrete bins; $130/ton for Recycling, Source Separated, Non-Recyclable, or Demolition bins over stated limits.
  • Metal-Only bins are not charged overweight fees and are credited based on market value minus transportation.
  • Driver standby fee of $150/hr applies if bin is not ready when dispatched (waived for delays under 20 minutes). After-hours service requests subject to additional charges.
  • Commingled Recycling bins must contain at least 65% recyclable material by weight and volume. Single-Item bins must contain 95%+ of a single item or will be reclassified.
  • Hazardous/Universal Solid Waste not accepted.
  • FIC fee (Facility Incoming Material Charge, currently $35.00) included on each invoice per service.
  • Prices are valid for 30 days and are for their respective project only. After 30 days, pricing may be subject to change. Tax rates and jurisdiction fees may change without notice. City of Los Gatos taxes will be applied to Proposal #1961.
