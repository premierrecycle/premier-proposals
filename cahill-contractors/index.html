Why It Matters Who You Choose

Fully Integrated vs. Fragmented

Not all partners are the same:

Fully Integrated
Premier Recycle
  • ✓
    One company owns the full chain. We operate the trucks and the sorting facility. Material goes directly from your site to our facility. No subcontractors. No hand-offs. No guessing where your material ended up.
  • ✓
    Service you can count on. We answer the phone, show up when we say we will, and solve problems quickly because we control the entire operation, from pickup through processing.
  • ✓
    Diversion reporting without the headache. Every load is weighed and recorded at our facility under your project. You request the documentation for Green Halo; we produce it.
  • ✓
    No surprises at closeout. Because we process the material ourselves, there are no missing tonnages, conflicting diversion tickets, or last-minute scrambling to piece together documentation from multiple vendors.
Hauler Only
No Facility, No Chain of Custody
  • ✗
    Material goes somewhere — you're just not sure where. Haulers take debris to whichever facility has capacity. You don't know where the material landed, or what the actual diversion rate is, until documentation starts getting requested.
  • ✗
    Good luck finding the right person. The hauler handles pickups. The facility handles reporting. When something goes wrong, you're stuck coordinating between multiple companies that each own only part of the process.
  • ✗
    Documentation comes from all directions. Each facility has its own format, timeline, and process. Suddenly your PM is sorting through a stack of tickets trying to figure out what belongs where.
  • ✗
    Advanced diversion goals require more than a hauler. Projects pursuing LEED certification often require a level of documentation, tracking, and coordination that haulers are not equipped to provide.
The Hidden Cost of Hauler-Only Providers
When Your Hauler Has No Facility,
Your Team Becomes the Documentation Manager

A hauler-only provider picks up your bin and disappears. Where the material goes — and what happens to the data — is no longer their problem. At close-out, that becomes your project team's problem: chasing weight tickets from multiple facilities, reconciling mismatched formats, and manually building the Green Halo report that was supposed to be someone else's job.

Terms & Conditions
  • Overweight charges: $100/ton for Concrete bins; $130/ton for Recycling, Source Separated, Non-Recyclable, or Demolition bins over stated limits.
  • Metal-Only bins are not charged overweight fees and are credited based on market value minus transportation.
  • Driver standby fee of $150/hr applies if bin is not ready when dispatched (waived for delays under 20 minutes). After-hours service requests subject to additional charges.
  • Commingled Recycling bins must contain at least 65% recyclable material by weight and volume. Single-Item bins must contain 95%+ of a single item or will be reclassified.
  • Hazardous/Universal Solid Waste not accepted.
  • FIC fee (Facility Incoming Material Charge) included on each invoice per service.
  • Pricing is project-specific and valid for 30 days from date of formal proposal. Tax rates and jurisdiction fees may change without notice.
