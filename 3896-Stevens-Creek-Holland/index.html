Why It Matters Who You Choose

Fully Integrated vs. Fragmented

Not all recyclers are the same:

The Gold Standard
Premier Recycle
  • ✓
    One company owns the full chain. We operate the trucks and the sorting facility. Material goes directly from your site to our facility. No hand-offs, no guessing where your material ended up.
  • ✓
    Reliable service, every time. Real people answer your calls and our team treats your schedule like it's our own. We're local, so next-day service is standard.
  • ✓
    Diversion reporting you can actually verify. Every load is weighed and recorded at our facility under your project name. We keep detailed photo records of your material, so if a question comes up at closeout, you get a real answer. Plus, we're happy to manage your GreenHalo uploads — just say the word.
  • ✓
    SJ City-Certified CDD Facility. Our facility meets San Jose's requirements for C&D debris diversion, a critical requirement for getting your deposit back from Environmental Services.
The Alternative
Hauler-Only Provider
  • ✗
    Material goes somewhere, you're just not sure where. Haulers take debris to whichever facility has capacity. You won't know the actual diversion rate until documentation gets requested at closeout.
  • ✗
    Multiple vendors, no single owner. The hauler handles pickups. A different facility handles reporting. If something goes wrong, you're coordinating between multiple companies.
  • ✗
    Documentation comes from all directions. Every facility has its own format and timeline. Your project team ends up sorting through a stack of tickets trying to figure out what belongs where.
  • ✗
    Service is inconsistent. Out-of-area dispatch means slower response times and drivers unfamiliar with your site. How fast they show up depends on how busy they are, not how important your project is.

Terms & Conditions
  • Overweight charges: $100/ton for Concrete bins; $130/ton for Recycling, Source Separated, Non-Recyclable, or Demolition bins over stated limits.
  • Metal-Only bins are not charged overweight fees and are credited based on market value minus transportation costs.
  • Driver standby fee of $150/hr applies if bin is not ready when dispatched (waived for delays under 20 minutes). After-hours service requests subject to additional charges.
  • Additional charges apply for cover-top debris bin services on a per-service basis (current rate $150.00 per service).
  • Commingled Recycling bins must contain at least 65% recyclable material by weight and volume. Single-Item bins must contain 95%+ of a single item or will be reclassified.
  • Hazardous/Universal Solid Waste not accepted.
  • FIC fee (Facility Incoming Material Charge, currently $40.00) included on each invoice per service.
  • Prices are valid for 30 days and are for this project only. After 30 days, pricing may be subject to change. Tax rates and jurisdiction fees may change without notice.
